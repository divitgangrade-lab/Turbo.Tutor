Refund &
cancellation

This page explains how money is returned if a session does not go ahead. It applies to tutoring arranged through TurboTutor and is part of the terms and conditions.

Effective 7 November 2026

// Click the surface to shove the pit and send a pulse through the map.

No payment is taken on this website. There is no checkout, no card field and no payment processor. Payment is arranged directly with the tutor by cash or Zelle after the schedule and final amount are confirmed. Refunds are therefore handled directly by the tutor, not through an online account.

1. Before you pay

The tutor confirms the subject, grade, session length, schedule, format and final amount in writing before anything is booked. Read that confirmation before paying. If something in it is wrong, say so before the session or before sending money. Prices on the site are suggested rates, not a demand for payment.

2. If the family cancels or moves a session

  • Reschedule a confirmed session: a $10 fee applies. Tell the tutor as early as you can so the slot can be offered to someone else.
  • Miss a scheduled session without notice: a $15 fee applies.
  • Cancel a prepaid session before it starts: the amount paid for that session is refunded, unless the cancellation falls within the reschedule or no-show rules above.

These fees reflect the time reserved and are not a penalty. They are confirmed before enrollment and are arranged directly between the family and the tutor.

3. If the tutor cancels or moves a session

If the tutor needs to move a session, the tutor confirms a 15% credit toward the next request. If the tutor cancels and cannot offer a workable replacement, you may choose either that 15% credit or a refund of the amount paid for the cancelled session. The choice is yours, and the tutor will not insist on credit instead of a refund.

4. Unused sessions in a prepaid plan

If you have paid for several sessions in advance and decide to stop, the unused sessions are refunded at the price paid for them. Sessions already delivered are not refunded. Any discount you received stays applied to the sessions you used; the refund is simply the amount actually paid for the sessions you did not use.

5. Offers, credits and the welcome wheel

Wheel prizes and seasonal offers have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money. A credit is applied to a future request and expires with the offer it came from. If a refunded payment included a discount, the refund is the amount you actually paid, not the undiscounted list price.

6. How to ask for a refund

Email divit.gangrade@gmail.com or text +1 804 847 3236 with the parent or guardian name, the student's name, the date and amount paid, and what you are asking for. The tutor aims to reply within five business days. If a refund is approved, it is paid by the same method you used, normally within 14 days of approval. Cash refunds are handed over in person; Zelle refunds are sent to the number or address the payment came from.

If you paid someone other than the tutor, tell the tutor so the payment can be traced. Nobody else is authorised to collect money for TurboTutor.

7. Disagreements

If you disagree with a fee or a refund decision, say so before paying the disputed amount. The tutor will explain the calculation and, where a mistake was made, correct it. Virginia consumers who cannot resolve a complaint directly may contact the Virginia Attorney General's Office of Consumer Affairs.

8. Your statutory rights

This policy describes the service's own rules. It does not reduce any right you have under the mandatory consumer law of your state or country, including any right to cancel a distance contract or to a remedy for a service that was not provided with reasonable care. If a statutory right gives you more than this policy, that right applies.

9. Changes to this policy

If these rules change, this page will be updated and the effective date at the top will change. The version in force when you paid applies to that payment unless you agree to a change.
