Terms, privacy
& payments

This page explains what this website does with your details, how money changes hands, and what happens when a session moves. It applies to the TurboTutor site and to tutoring requests made through it.

Effective 6 November 2026

No online payment

TurboTutor does not take payments on this website. There are no card fields, no checkout and no payment processor. The kart builds a request and opens it as a draft in your own email or messaging app. You review it and send it yourself.

After the tutor confirms availability, schedule and the final amount, payment is arranged directly by cash or Zelle. Zelle details are shared privately by the tutor, never collected here.

The prices shown are suggested rates. Nothing is charged, reserved or enrolled until the tutor confirms the details with you and you agree.

What a request contains

When you prepare a request, the form asks for a parent or guardian name, the student's name, an email address, a phone number, the plans you selected, your preferred payment method and an acknowledgement that you read the attendance policy. That information is assembled in your browser and written into the draft message. It is not submitted to a TurboTutor server, because this site has no server or database.

If you choose email, the draft opens in your email app addressed to divit.gangrade@gmail.com. If you choose text, it opens in your messaging app addressed to +1 804 847 3236. Nothing is sent until you press send in that app.

What stays in your browser

The site uses local browser storage for small conveniences: your light or dark theme choice, the contents of your kart, the date of a wheel spin so the same offer is not spun twice, and a flag that skips the opening animation on a return visit. These values stay on your device and are not sent anywhere.

There are no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, session recordings or third-party cookies on this site. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser details as part of serving the font files. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs for security and delivery.

Children and guardians

Sessions are requested by a parent or guardian, not by the student. A parent or guardian must remain present for in-person sessions with students under 18. Please share only the student details needed to plan lessons.

Attendance, reschedules and fees

  • A missed scheduled session without notice carries a $15 fee.
  • A family-requested reschedule of a confirmed session carries a $10 fee.
  • If the tutor needs to move a session, the tutor confirms a 15% credit toward the next request.

These are proposed service terms shown before enrolment. They are arranged directly between the family and the tutor, and this website never charges them.

Offers and the wheel

Wheel and seasonal offers are optional, require no purchase, and are limited to one spin per browser every 30 days for a parent or guardian aged 18 or over. Published odds appear beside the wheel. Cash discounts do not stack; the best eligible cash discount applies. Priority scheduling and the Zoom introduction are non-cash and depend on availability. Offers expire 30 days after they are revealed.

Team gear

The gear store is under construction. Concepts shown on the site are not products, carry no price and cannot be ordered. Registering interest only opens an email draft to the tutor.

Your choices

Because the site stores your request only in your own apps, the copy of that message lives with your email or messaging provider. You can clear the theme, kart and spin records at any time by clearing site data in your browser.

To ask what tutoring records are held, to correct a detail, or to ask for deletion, email divit.gangrade@gmail.com or text +1 804 847 3236. The tutor replies to requests, corrections and deletion questions directly.

Changes

If these terms or the way the site handles data change, the effective date above changes with them. Material changes will be described on this page before they apply to a new request.
