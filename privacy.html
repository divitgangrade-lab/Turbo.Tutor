TurboTutor is a trading name used by Divit Gangrade for independent tutoring. This site does not publish a company registration number or a registered office. If a separate legal entity is used for a booking, the tutor will tell you in writing before you pay anything.

The short version. This is a static website. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no online payment. A request, message or club signup is sent from this site through FormSubmit, a form-to-email processor, to the tutor's inbox. The only personal information the tutor receives is what you choose to submit.

1. What this policy covers

This policy applies to turbotutor.dpdns.org and to enquiries, tutoring and messages that follow from it. The licence that covers tutoring materials, and the way copyright complaints are handled, are set out in the end user licence agreement and the copyright and DMCA policy. This policy does not cover websites run by other organisations, including the Wikimedia Commons and Creative Commons pages linked from the photo credits, the Henrico County library booking page, or Zoom. Those services have their own privacy policies.

2. Information you give

The request form asks for a parent or guardian name, the student's name, an email address, a phone number, the plans you selected, your preferred payment method, and an acknowledgement of the policies. That information is needed to reply to you, check availability, plan sessions and agree a price. If you leave it out, the tutor cannot prepare a request.

The Paddock Club form asks for an email address and your agreement to these documents. Direct emails, text messages and calls contain whatever you choose to include.

Please do not send Social Security numbers, government identification, payment card numbers, bank details, passwords, medical records or other sensitive information. None of it is needed to arrange tutoring, and email and text are not secure ways to send it.

3. How a request leaves your browser

There is no TurboTutor server or database behind the forms. When you choose Send, the details go from your browser to FormSubmit, which forwards them to the tutor's email address. FormSubmit is a form-to-email processor; TurboTutor does not keep a copy on this website. The details are not stored in browser storage after you send them. FormSubmit says it keeps submissions for up to 30 days while delivering them.

Once you send it, the message is delivered through your email provider or mobile carrier and arrives in the tutor's email inbox or phone. The tutor uses it to reply and to keep a record of what was agreed.

4. Children and students

The site is intended for parents and guardians aged 18 or over. Students do not create accounts and the site does not ask them to submit anything. A parent or guardian sends the request and remains responsible for the information in it.

Please share only what is needed to plan lessons: a first name, the grade, the subject, the session format and any learning needs the tutor should know about. The tutor does not knowingly collect personal information directly from a child under 13. If you believe a child has sent information without a parent or guardian, contact the tutor and it will be deleted. In person, a parent or guardian must remain present for students under 18.

5. Cookies and browser storage

This site does not set cookies. It stores two small preferences in your own browser so that features work as expected: your light or dark theme choice, and the record of a welcome-offer spin so the same offer is not revealed twice. The cookie policy lists them, explains why they are functional rather than tracking, and shows how to clear them.

6. Analytics, advertising and embeds

There are no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, heat maps, session recordings, social plugins or third-party iframes on this site. Fonts, images, styles and scripts are served from this domain, so loading a page does not send your IP address to a font network or an embed provider. The only cross-origin request is made when you choose to send a form: it goes to FormSubmit.co so the message can reach the tutor's inbox. Outbound links open another site only when you choose them.

The site does not track you across other websites, does not build a profile of you and does not make automated decisions about you. Because there is no tracking, a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal has nothing to switch off here.

7. Hosting and service providers

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. in the United States. Like any web host, GitHub processes technical request data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. GitHub handles that data under its own privacy statement.

The tutor also relies on an email provider, a mobile carrier, and Zoom for online sessions. Those providers process messages and meeting data under their own terms. Personal information is not sold, rented or shared for advertising. It is shared only with those providers as needed to run tutoring, or where the law requires it.

FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) is the form-to-email processor used by the request, contact and Paddock Club forms. It receives the name, email address, phone number, student details, message and any other information you enter, and forwards it to the tutor's email address. FormSubmit says it retains submissions for up to 30 days while delivering them and handles them under its privacy policy. Do not send identity documents, card numbers or medical records through these forms.

8. How long information is kept

  • An enquiry that does not lead to tutoring is deleted within 12 months of the last message.
  • Student and session records are kept while tutoring continues and for up to 12 months after the last session, then deleted.
  • A Paddock Club email address is kept until you ask to be removed.
  • Records may be kept longer where tax, accounting or other law requires it, or to establish or defend a legal claim.
  • Messages may remain in provider backups for a limited period after deletion, under the provider's own schedule.

9. International transfers

The tutor is in the United States and the providers named above may process data there. If you contact the tutor from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region with transfer rules, the transfer relies on the providers' standard contractual clauses or another lawful mechanism. Contact the tutor if you want more detail about the safeguard that applies.

10. Your rights

You can ask what personal information is held about you, ask for a copy, ask for a correction, ask for deletion, or object to how it is used. Email divit.gangrade@gmail.com or text +1 804 847 3236. The tutor may ask for enough information to confirm who is making the request, and will respond within the time the applicable law allows, normally 30 to 45 days.

Virginia

If you are a Virginia consumer, the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act gives you the right to confirm whether your personal data is processed, to access it, to correct it, to delete it, to obtain a copy, and to appeal a refusal. TurboTutor does not sell personal data, does not use it for targeted advertising and does not profile you. To appeal a decision, reply to the tutor's response with the word "appeal". You may also complain to the Virginia Attorney General's Office of Consumer Affairs.

California

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended gives you the right to know what personal information is collected, to request a copy, to correct it, to delete it, and to be free from discrimination for exercising those rights. TurboTutor does not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by that law, and has not done so in the past 12 months. You may use an authorised agent, with proof of authorisation.

European Economic Area and United Kingdom

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the lawful basis for handling a request is to take steps at your request before entering a contract, and the legitimate interest in replying to enquiries and running tutoring. Marketing emails rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. You have the rights listed above, plus the right to restrict processing, the right to data portability and the right to complain to your local supervisory authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office.

11. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Because it has no login, no database and no payment form, there is very little to breach. The main risk is the ordinary one: email and text messages can be intercepted or misdirected. Send only what is necessary, and tell the tutor straight away if you think a message went to the wrong person. If a breach affects your information and the law requires notice, the tutor will contact you and the relevant authority without undue delay.

12. Links to other sites

Pages on this site link to Wikimedia Commons, Creative Commons, the Henrico County library booking system, GitHub and Zoom. Those links are there for reference. Once you follow one, the destination's privacy policy applies, not this one.

13. Changes to this policy

If the way the site handles information changes, this page will be updated and the effective date at the top will change. A material change will be described on this page before it applies to new requests. Previous versions are not archived online, but you can ask the tutor for the version that applied when you sent a request.

14. Contact

Questions, requests and complaints about privacy go to Divit Gangrade at divit.gangrade@gmail.com or +1 804 847 3236. If a complaint is not resolved, you may contact the consumer protection office in your state or country. This page describes the site's practices and is not legal advice.
