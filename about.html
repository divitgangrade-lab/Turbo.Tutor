The tutor behind TurboTutor

TurboTutor is a one-to-one tutoring service for Grades 1-8 in Math, English and Science. It is run by Divit Gangrade, online via Zoom and in person at Twin Hickory Library in Henrico County, Virginia.

Meet Divit

Divit Gangrade is the tutor behind TurboTutor. Divit works one-to-one with students in Grades 1-8 across Math, English and Science, building each session around the work the student is actually doing at school.

The approach is simple: start with what the student already knows, work through the next idea together, then give the student room to drive. Sessions are short enough to stay focused and long enough to finish the thought.

There is no cold checkout here. You see the session length, the number of sessions and the suggested price before you request anything. The tutor confirms the schedule, the meeting place and the final amount directly.

What is not published

There are no testimonials, star ratings or invented student results on this site. Those claims are not published because the site does not manufacture proof. The plan details, terms and contact route are here so you can judge the fit before you request a session.
