Inside one 45-minute lap

A real lesson structure, not a testimonial. Student names and results are never published on this site.

Run sheet45 minutes · one student · one tutor

  1. 00–05

    Warm-up

    Two quick questions from last week's work. This shows what stayed and what needs another look before anything new starts.

    5 min
  2. 05–20

    The concept

    One idea at a time, worked in front of the student, then repeated back in their own words. No worksheet dump.

    15 min
  3. 20–40

    Guided practice

    The student drives. The tutor watches the working, catches the first wrong turn, and asks the question that fixes it.

    20 min
  4. 40–45

    Recap and next lap

    Three things that clicked, one thing to practise, and the plan for the next session. Sent in writing after the lap.

    5 min

What the family gets: the lap length, the plan and the full price before the session, then a short written summary after it. No card and no online payment at any point.

11 answers

What grades and subjects can my student choose?

Grades 1 through 8, in Science, English or Math. You can add more than one course to a single kart request, and each one keeps its own lap length and location.

How much does tutoring cost?

Weekly sessions start at $24 for a 45-minute Grade 1 Science lap on Zoom. Math adds $3 and English adds $5 per weekly lap. A public-library session adds $10, a 30-minute lap is about 30% less, a 60-minute lap is about 50% more, and rates rise gently from Grade 1 to Grade 8. Monthly blocks are 5% lower than paying weekly and the yearly plan is 15% lower than 48 weekly laps. Every price appears before you add anything to the kart.

What does each plan include?

Weekly includes one class per week, Monthly includes four, and Monthly is 5% lower than paying weekly, and Yearly includes 48 sessions across 12 months at 15% off. Every plan lets you pick a 30-, 45- or 60-minute lap. The 60-minute option is the recommended one because it leaves room for guided practice and a proper wrap-up.

How do online and library sessions work?

Online sessions meet on Zoom and the tutor confirms the link after enrollment. In-person laps meet at Twin Hickory Library in Henrico County and cost $10 more per 45-minute lap. Rooms are booked through the library's room page. A parent or guardian must remain present for students under 18.

What are the special coaching programs?

Moody IB Prep is a 120-minute Zoom session at $120. The High School Application Review is $35 per application, with an optional 90-minute Zoom meeting for $40 more. Both are one-time requests; the tutor confirms scope and availability first.

Does the kart take payment?

No. The kart is just a build sheet: it collects the plans you want and turns them into a tidy email or text request. No card fields, no checkout, no online payment anywhere on this site.

How do the seasonal offers work?

The page checks the calendar and rotates the offer for New Year, back-to-school, Diwali season (Oct 1–Nov 10), Thanksgiving week and Christmas. Only one cash discount applies per request: the best eligible seasonal or wheel discount wins.

What happens if we miss or move a class?

A missed scheduled class carries a $15 fee. A family-requested reschedule carries a $10 fee. If the tutor needs to move a session, they confirm a 15% credit toward the next request. These terms are shown before enrollment and the website never charges them.

How do I request enrollment?

Add your plans to the kart, enter the parent/guardian and student details, choose cash or Zelle as the preferred method, then send the request. It goes straight to the tutor's inbox from this page, and the tutor replies to confirm.

Can I pay by cash or Zelle?

Yes. Pick cash or Zelle in the request form. The tutor confirms the amount, due date and Zelle details directly with you before any enrollment. This site never collects card numbers or online payments.
