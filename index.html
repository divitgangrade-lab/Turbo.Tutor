Session walkthrough

Inside one 45-minute lap

A real lesson structure, not a testimonial. Student names and results are never published on this site.

Run sheet45 minutes · one student · one tutor

  1. 00–05

    Warm-up

    Two quick questions from last week's work. This shows what stayed and what needs another look before anything new starts.

    5 min
  2. 05–20

    The concept

    One idea at a time, worked in front of the student, then repeated back in their own words. No worksheet dump.

    15 min
  3. 20–40

    Guided practice

    The student drives. The tutor watches the working, catches the first wrong turn, and asks the question that fixes it.

    20 min
  4. 40–45

    Recap and next lap

    Three things that clicked, one thing to practise, and the plan for the next session. Sent in writing after the lap.

    5 min

What the family gets: the lap length, the plan and the full price before the session, then a short written summary after it. No card and no online payment at any point.

11 answers

What grades and subjects can my student choose?

Grades 1 through 8, in Science, English or Math. You can add more than one course to a single kart request, and each one keeps its own lap length and location.

How much does tutoring cost?

Weekly sessions start at $30 for a 45-minute Grade 1 Science lap on Zoom. Math adds $4 and English adds $7 per weekly lap. A public-library session adds $15, a 30-minute lap is about 30% less, a 60-minute lap is about 60% more, and rates rise gently from Grade 1 to Grade 8. Every price appears before you add anything to the kart.

What does each plan include?

Weekly includes one class per week, Monthly includes four, and Yearly includes 48 across 12 months at 10% off. Every plan lets you pick a 30-, 45- or 60-minute lap. The 60-minute option is the recommended one because it leaves room for guided practice and a proper wrap-up.

How do online and library sessions work?

Online sessions meet on Zoom and the tutor confirms the link after enrollment. In-person sessions meet at a mutually agreed public library and cost $15 more per 45-minute lap. A parent or guardian must remain present for students under 18.

What are the special coaching programs?

Moody IB Prep is a 60-minute advanced session at $150, and the High School Application Review is a 60-minute draft review at $65. Both are one-time requests; the listed rates are suggested starting prices and the tutor confirms scope and availability first.

Does the kart take payment?

No. The kart is just a build sheet: it collects the plans you want and turns them into a tidy email or text request. No card fields, no checkout, no online payment anywhere on this site.

When can I buy team gear?

Not yet. The paddock shop is under construction: three concepts are in the pipeline and nothing is for sale. Register your interest and you will hear about drop 01 when it exists.

How do the seasonal offers work?

The page checks the calendar and rotates the offer for New Year, back-to-school, Diwali season (Oct 1–Nov 10), Thanksgiving week and Christmas. Only one cash discount applies per request: the best eligible seasonal or wheel discount wins.

What happens if we miss or move a class?

A missed scheduled class carries a $25 fee. A family-requested reschedule carries a $15 fee. If the tutor needs to move a session, they confirm a 15% credit toward the next request. These terms are shown before enrollment and the website never charges them.

How do I request enrollment?

Add your plans to the kart, enter the parent/guardian and student details, choose cash or Zelle as the preferred method, then prepare the request. Your email or messaging app opens with everything filled in. Review it, press send, and the tutor replies to confirm.

Can I pay by cash or Zelle?

Yes. Pick cash or Zelle in the request form. The tutor confirms the amount, due date and Zelle details directly with you before any enrollment. This site never collects card numbers or online payments.
