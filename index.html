Quick answers

Before the start

9 answers

What subjects can my student choose?

Choose Science, English, or Math for any grade from 1 through 8. You can add more than one course to a single request.

What does each plan include?

The weekly plan includes one class per week, the monthly plan includes four, and the yearly plan includes 48 across 12 months. Choose a 30-, 45-, or 60-minute class for every plan. The 60-minute option includes extra time for guided practice and feedback.

How are prices set?

Rates rise by grade. At 45 minutes on Zoom, Science is the base rate, Math adds $5, and English adds $10 per weekly class. In-person library sessions add $20 per 45-minute class. The 60-minute option has a premium rate shown before you add it.

How do online and library sessions work?

Online sessions meet on Zoom. In-person sessions meet at a mutually agreed public library and cost more than Zoom. A parent or guardian must remain present for students under 18. Confirm availability before enrollment.

What are the specialty programs?

Moody IB prep, high school application review, and Maggie prep are separate one-time programs. Their listed rates are suggested; contact the tutor to confirm the exact scope, materials, and appointment availability.

How do seasonal offers update?

The page checks the calendar and rotates its offer automatically for New Year, back-to-school, Diwali season (Oct 1-Nov 10), Thanksgiving week, and Christmas. Only one cash discount applies per request: the best eligible seasonal or wheel discount.

What if someone misses or reschedules a class?

A missed scheduled class has a $25 fee. A family-requested reschedule has a $15 fee. If the tutor must reschedule, they confirm a 15% credit for the student's next purchase request. These terms are shown before enrollment; the website does not charge fees or payments.

How do I request enrollment?

Add courses, enter your contact details and payment preference, then choose an email or text draft. Your email or messaging app opens with the request filled in. Review it and press Send to contact the tutor. No charge is made by this site.

Can I pay by cash or Zelle?

Select cash or Zelle in the request form. No payment is collected on this website; confirm payment instructions directly with the tutor.
