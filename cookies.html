No cookies are set. This site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising identifiers or cross-site tracking. There is nothing to opt into, so there is no cookie banner.

1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small file a website asks your browser to store and send back with later requests. Cookies can remember a login, measure how a page is used, or follow a visitor between sites. Browser storage, such as localStorage and sessionStorage, is a related mechanism that keeps small values on your device without sending them with every request.

2. Cookies used by this site

None. The site does not create cookies, does not read cookies set by anyone else, and loads no third-party script that could do so. You can confirm this in your browser's developer tools under Application, Cookies.

3. Browser storage used by this site

Two small values are stored locally so that a feature works the way you expect. Neither is sent to the tutor, to GitHub, or to anyone else.

A pit-wall note is also not saved in browser storage: it is sent to the review API and stored publicly on the pit wall.

4. Why no consent banner is needed

Cookie consent rules, including the ePrivacy Directive in the European Union and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations in the United Kingdom, require consent for storing or reading information on your device unless the storage is strictly necessary to provide a service you asked for.

The two values above are used only to deliver features you choose on this site: your theme and a single welcome offer. They do not identify you, do not follow you to other sites, do not feed a profile and are not used for advertising or measurement. That makes them functional storage rather than tracking, so the site treats them as exempt and shows no banner. The site also sets no cookies at all, which is the simplest way to keep the position clear.

If you would rather not have this storage, you can block site data for this domain in your browser. The site still works. Your theme returns to the default and the wheel may appear again.

5. Analytics, advertising and third parties

There is no Google Analytics, no advertising pixel, no heat map, no session recording and no social plugin on this site. Fonts and images are served from this domain rather than a third-party network, so no font or image provider receives your request. Cross-origin requests are made only when you submit a form: request, contact and club messages go to FormSubmit.co, and a pit-wall note goes to the review API. Links to other sites, such as the library booking page or Wikimedia Commons, do not load anything from those sites until you click them.

GitHub Pages, which hosts the site, keeps standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and time of request for delivery and security. Those logs are not cookies and are not used by TurboTutor for analytics. See the privacy policy for detail.

6. How to clear this storage

  • Chrome on a computer: Settings, Privacy and security, Third-party cookies, See all site data and permissions, search for the site, then delete.
  • Safari on a computer: Settings, Privacy, Manage Website Data, select the site, then Remove.
  • Firefox: Settings, Privacy and Security, Cookies and Site Data, Manage Data, select the site, then Remove Selected.
  • Edge: Settings, Cookies and site permissions, Manage and delete cookies and site data, then remove the site.
  • On a phone or tablet: open the browser's site settings for this domain and choose Clear data, or clear browsing data for all sites.

Clearing site data does not affect a request you have already sent. FormSubmit has already passed it to the tutor's inbox, and clearing this browser will not recall it. It also does not remove a public pit-wall note; ask the tutor to delete that if you want it removed.

7. Changes to this policy

If the site ever adds a cookie, an embed or a measurement tool, this page will list it before it is used, the effective date will change, and consent will be requested where the law requires it. The site will not add silent tracking.
