# TurboTutor backlink and citation strategy

This file is internal. `seo/` is excluded from the published GitHub Pages site in `_config.yml`.

## Position

TurboTutor is a one-to-one tutoring service for Grades 1-8 in Richmond, Glen Allen and Henrico County, Virginia. It is delivered on Zoom or at Twin Hickory Library, costs from $24 a week, and takes no online payment. Every link target should make sense for a parent, teacher or local organisation in that position.

Google does not rank a new site because it exists. Links and citations are how other sites vouch for it. The work below earns those mentions through relevance and usefulness, not volume.

## Goals for the first 90 days

- 10 to 15 relevant referring domains, at least half local or education-focused.
- 5 to 8 consistent local citations with the same name, phone, email and URL.
- 9 indexed public pages at launch, growing to 10 or more as the first resource pages are added.
- Referral traffic and enquiries that can be traced with UTM links.
- No paid links, private blog networks, mass guest posts or automated directory spam.

Track referring domains, not raw link count. One link from a Henrico parent resource page is worth more than fifty low-quality directory listings.

Priority profiles:

1. **Google Business Profile** as a service-area business. Categories: tutoring service, math tutor, English tutor, science tutor. Use the real service area, not a fake storefront. Add the site, phone, hours, description and photos. This is a citation and a local ranking surface.
2. **Bing Places** and **Apple Business Connect** with the same details.
3. **Nextdoor business page** for the Richmond and Glen Allen neighbourhoods, if eligible.
4. **Henrico County Public Library** community or tutor resource pages. Twin Hickory Library is already the in-person location, so ask the branch about a community listing or a free study-skills workshop.
5. **Virginia homeschool and parent organisations**, such as HEAV and local co-op resource lists, where a tutoring listing is allowed.
6. **Henrico Chamber of Commerce** and any Glen Allen / Short Pump community business directory with a free tier.
7. **Tutoring marketplaces** where a profile is allowed and does not undercut the direct offer: Wyzant, Varsity Tutors, Care.com, Superprof, Thumbtack, Outschool (online only). Use them for discovery and citations, not as the main funnel.
8. **Local parenting and school channels**: PTA newsletters, school newspaper business directories, sports club and robotics club sponsor pages.

Keep a single source of truth for the details above. Inconsistent phone numbers or URLs weaken citations.

## Linkable assets to build

These are the pages other people can cite without being asked to promote a business:

- A printable **Virginia SOL study checklist** by grade and subject.
- A **grade-by-grade math skills checklist** for Grades 1-8.
- A simple **tutoring cost guide for Richmond** explaining weekly, monthly and yearly pricing and what changes the price.
- A **free 10-question diagnostic** that produces a short topic list, with no email gate.
- A **parent guide to a first Zoom tutoring session**: what to prepare, how long it takes, how progress is reported.
- A **weekly session planner** PDF that works for any subject.

Publish each as a real page or downloadable file with a clear title, a short explanation and a canonical URL. These earn links because they are useful, not because they mention TurboTutor.

## Digital PR and content-led links

- Run a small, honest survey of local parents about homework time and confidence. Publish the aggregate results with the method and sample size. Local journalists and parent groups can cite it.
- Publish a **Richmond tutoring price comparison** based only on public, verifiable prices, with the date checked. Update it quarterly.
- Offer short expert commentary to education and parenting writers on study routines, Zoom fatigue and SOL preparation. Link back only when the piece is genuinely published.
- Pitch podcast appearances for local parent and education shows. Show notes links are durable.
- Write one substantial guest post per quarter for a parent, homeschool or local-news site. No link stuffing and no mass guest posting.

## Anchor text and link rules

- Prefer the brand ("TurboTutor") or the URL. Use descriptive anchors such as "one-to-one tutoring in Richmond" sparingly and only when they read naturally.
- Never buy links, join link schemes, use private blog networks or exchange links at scale.
- No exact-match anchor blocks in guest posts. One contextual link per piece is enough.
- Ask for a link only where the page genuinely helps the reader. Do not ask for a link on a page that has nothing to do with tutoring.
- Keep a record of every request, even the ones that say no.

## Tracking

Use a spreadsheet with one row per target:

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Target | twin-hickory-library |
| Page URL | https://.../homework-help |
| Contact | branch manager |
| Date contacted | 2026-11-10 |
| Status | contacted / replied / live / declined |
| Live URL | https://... |
| Anchor | TurboTutor |
| Domain type | local / education / directory / press |
| Referral sessions | from analytics |
| Enquiries | from the request form or email |
| Next follow-up | 2026-11-24 |

For campaign links, use UTM parameters on the URL you hand out:

`?utm_source=library&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-skills-2026`

Do not put UTM parameters on the canonical URL of a page; use them only on the shared link.

## 90-day schedule

**Weeks 1-2**
- Finish Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect and Nextdoor.
- Ask Twin Hickory Library about a workshop and a resource listing.
- Build the SOL checklist and the session planner.

**Weeks 3-6**
- Publish the cost guide and the parent guide to a first session.
- Send 8 to 10 local outreach emails over four weeks.
- Submit to three relevant education or homeschool directories.
- Follow up once after 10 to 14 days.

**Weeks 7-10**
- Run the parent survey or publish the price comparison.
- Pitch two local publications and two podcasts.
- Offer one guest post to a parent or homeschool site.
- Ask two partner organisations for sponsor or resource-page mentions.

**Weeks 11-13**
- Review which pages earned links and which emails were ignored.
- Update the price guide and checklist with the current date.
- Keep the five best-performing outreach angles and drop the rest.
- Set next quarter's target: ten more relevant referring domains.

## What success looks like

Search Console will show new referring links under Links, and Analytics or the site's own enquiry log will show referral sessions. Rankings usually move after the pages are indexed and links are discovered, not on the day a link goes live. Recheck the sitemap and canonical setup with `npm run seo` after every content change.
